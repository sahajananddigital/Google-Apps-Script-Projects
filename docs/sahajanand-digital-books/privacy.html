Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 20, 2026 • Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Gurukul Books - Invoicing & Accounting ERP ("we", "our", or "the Application"). We are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and financial sovereignty of our users.

Gurukul Books is architected as a decentralized, private Google Workspace Web Application. Unlike traditional cloud SaaS products that store your financial ledgers and customer records on centralized third-party servers, Gurukul Books operates entirely within your own Google Account ecosystem. Your spreadsheets remain 100% in your own Google Drive.

🛡️

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Gurukul Books' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

2. Google Account Permissions & OAuth Scopes

To provide invoicing, general ledger accounting, receipt generation, and live financial reconciliations, Gurukul Books requests explicit authorization for the following minimal OAuth scopes:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Purpose (Least Privilege): To create and manage ONLY the specific accounting database spreadsheet created by this application in your Google Drive. Gurukul Books cannot read, modify, or view any of your other personal or corporate files.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets.currentonly

Purpose: To interact strictly with the active spreadsheet when the user opens the Gurukul Books add-on within Google Sheets.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

Purpose: To authenticate your session and isolate your preferences securely.

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/script.external_request

Purpose: Used solely if enabled to fetch live currency exchange rates or perform offline client-side synchronization. No user financial data is transmitted.

3. Data Storage & Zero Third-Party Transmission

  • No External Database: We do NOT operate an external database, backend database cluster, or telemetry server. All accounting entries, customer personal information, and business records are stored solely in your personal or organizational Google Drive.
  • No Data Monetization or Sale: We never sell, lease, monetize, share, or broker your personal information or financial data to advertisers, data aggregators, or third parties.
  • No AI Model Training: Your Google Sheets data, invoices, student fees, or accounting numbers are NEVER used to train machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • Direct Client-to-Sheets Protocol: Invocations made from the web interface interact directly with your authorized Google Apps Script endpoints without passing through any intermediary proxy.

4. User Rights, Data Control & Deletion

Because your data is stored in your personal Google Sheets document, you retain 100% control at all times:

  • Full Exportability: You can export your data at any time to CSV, Excel (.xlsx), or PDF directly via Google Sheets or the built-in backup tools.
  • Instant Data Deletion: You can delete any individual transaction or remove the entire Google Spreadsheet database directly in Google Drive.
  • Revoking Access: You can instantly revoke Gurukul Books' access to your Google Account at any time by visiting your Google Account Third-Party Permissions page.

5. Security & Infrastructure

Gurukul Books inherits the enterprise-grade physical, network, and application security of Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace. All transmissions are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptography, and data is encrypted at rest by Google's infrastructure.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect application updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
