2. Description of Software & Architecture

Gurukul Books is an open-source, serverless management platform built using Google Apps Script and modern web technologies. It is designed to turn a standard Google Sheet into an automated ERP database supporting:

  • GST-compliant Tax Invoices and Educational Fee Receipts
  • Double-Entry General Ledgers and Journal Vouchers (JVs)
  • Live Bank & Cash Reconciliation and P&L / Balance Sheet reporting
  • Service and Course Catalog management with customizable units and currencies

The software runs directly in your browser and on your Google Apps Script environment. We do not host your database or process your private business records on any proprietary server.
